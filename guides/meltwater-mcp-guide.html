Deploying Meltwater MCP to your customers

Meltwater MCP in 60 seconds

What it is, what your customer can ask, and where it fits — before you deploy it.

The one-liner: Meltwater MCP brings your customer's Meltwater intelligence into the AI assistants their team already uses — Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok list Meltwater in their own catalogs, and most other MCP-compatible assistants can connect through a custom URL.4 They ask a question in plain language and get a cited answer grounded in their own Meltwater data, without logging into the platform or building an integration. It can also take action — creating and editing searches, drafting outreach — not just answer.6

MCP (Model Context Protocol) is the open standard that makes the connection work. It surfaces a set of capabilities that map to the products in the customer's plan:1

Ask & Brief

A cited overview or answer on a brand, topic, or event — a structured brief in minutes.

Coverage Analysis

Explore Meltwater metrics conversationally: volume, sentiment, trends, share of voice.

Manage Searches

Reference asset lists and saved Explore searches — and, with the right permissions, create and edit them.

Profiles & Influencers

People and creator intelligence — who's driving the conversation and whether they fit (Klear add-on).

Media Relations

Journalist and outlet intelligence — who's covering a topic and what angle lands (Media Relations add-on).

AI Visibility

How the brand shows up in AI-generated answers (GenAI Lens add-on).

Why customers trust the output: analytics are calculated on the customer's own Meltwater dataset and align with Explore. Depending on the prompt, the assistant may work from a representative sample, so when exact numbers matter, coach users to ask for specific metrics, searches, and timeframes.4 When there's no Meltwater data for a question, it returns nothing and shows the query it ran rather than inventing an answer, and it enforces each user's own Meltwater permissions.4
Scope of this guide: this covers the no-code, in-assistant connectors for Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok — the path your PR, comms, and marketing customers use. It is not the developer / custom-agent path (API token, custom connection URLs, other assistants); that's covered briefly under Troubleshooting & limits.
The deployment

The three-step activation journey

Almost every "it's not working" issue traces back to a step being skipped or done in the wrong order. Get these three in sequence and the rest is easy.

1
Implementation Manager

Get Meltwater ready

Confirm the plan and entitlements, add good saved searches, and make sure users have Meltwater seats.

2
Champion + IT admin

Enable at the org level

The customer's IT admin turns on the Meltwater connector or plugin for the organization.

3
End users

Connect + first skill

Each user connects their own account, verifies, and runs a Meltwater skill for a fast first win.

1

Get Meltwater ready

Owner: Implementation Manager

Your pre-flight check before you involve the customer. Run through this first — deploying into an account without the right entitlements or any saved searches is the fastest way to a flat demo.

  • MCP is on the subscription. A signed order that includes Meltwater MCP subscription – Intelligence Credits. Access to Meltwater on its own does not include MCP.4
  • A base Explore or Explore+ package is in place. It's a hard prerequisite — no Explore, no MCP.4
  • Supported assistant, enterprise tier. Claude Enterprise or Team, ChatGPT Enterprise or Business, or Grok (x.ai) — these list Meltwater in their own catalogs. Personal Pro / Plus accounts are much less likely to clear org approval — connectors and plugins are managed at the org level, and a personal account needs the heavier custom-connector setup rather than the native connector. Not a hard technical block, just a steep uphill climb.23
  • Sign-in method confirmed. Users sign in with their real Meltwater identity via SSO. They never enter credentials into the assistant.4
  • Add-on tools match the contract. Once the order is signed, core tools activate and the add-on tools on the contract — GenAI Lens, Media Relations, Klear — are assigned automatically. Confirm the add-ons you plan to demo are on the contract. Trials include core tools only.410
  • Credit entitlement is visible. An account admin should see MCP usage under the Account page (credits remaining, usage by user, top tools). If the dashboard is missing or shows no credits, file a Support ticket — it usually means the credit entitlement isn't mapped.12
  • At least one good saved Explore search. Not strictly required — live questions return results without one — but saved searches sharpen results and make citations render predictably. Set one up before you demo.4
Access comes with the Meltwater seat. MCP is account-wide: every user with a Meltwater seat gets the tools, and each user's platform permissions (including View-Only) carry over.4 Two things follow from that:
  • Account → Users is your MCP user list. Anyone who needs MCP needs a Meltwater seat. Adding a lot of users? Bulk upload via CSV or Excel handles up to 1,000 rows at a time.10
  • MCP can't be limited to a subset of users. Everyone with a seat gets it. Role-based access is on the roadmap; if a customer needs it, log it in #product-feedback.4
2

Enable at the org level

Owner: Champion + IT admin

This is the single org-level switch that turns Meltwater on inside the assistant. It's done once, by whoever owns Claude or ChatGPT for the organization. Your day-to-day champion usually isn't that person, so expect to arm them with what their IT / assistant admin needs.1

  • Who does it: a Claude Owner / Primary Owner, or a ChatGPT Workspace Owner / Admin — enabling it for everyone in the org.23
  • What they do: turn on the Meltwater connector (Claude) or the Meltwater plugin (ChatGPT) at the organization level, following Meltwater's IT admin guide.
  • Then it hands off: once it's enabled at the org level, anyone with a Meltwater seat can connect their own account (Step 3).
The IT admin steps are Meltwater-provided — point the admin to the one that matches their assistant: Connect Meltwater in Claude — IT admin steps or Connect Meltwater in ChatGPT — IT admin steps. For the security and compliance questions that come up here, share both the MCP Security & Compliance guide (MCP-specific detail) and the Meltwater Trust Center. If IT wants a one-pager, the shorter AI Security leave-behind PDF is on Saleshood. For assistants beyond Claude and ChatGPT (Grok, Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity), the developer portal has a connection guide for each.13

Most champions aren't technical and won't be able to answer IT's questions themselves. Send them this — it gives them everything to forward straight to their IT / assistant admin:

3

Connect + first skill

Owner: End users

With the org-level switch on, each user with a Meltwater seat connects their own account — about two minutes, and no credentials ever go into the assistant.1

Connect in Claude
Enterprise / Team
  1. Open Claude, signed in to the org's work account.
  2. Click Customize.
  3. In the search bar, type Meltwater.
  4. Click Connect on the Meltwater card.
  5. A browser window opens the Meltwater sign-in page — sign in with SSO.
  6. Click Authorize. Meltwater now shows as an active connector.
Verify: new chat → ask "List my saved Meltwater searches." The Meltwater icon appears, then a permission pop-up — choose Allow once or Always allow. Seeing your searches confirms it's live.2
Connect in ChatGPT
Enterprise / Business
  1. Open ChatGPT, signed in with the work account (not personal).
  2. Click Plugins from the sidebar.
  3. Search for Meltwater.
  4. Click Connect.
  5. A browser window opens the Meltwater sign-in page — sign in with SSO.
  6. Click Authorize. The plugin's actions appear (gear icon next to Start chat).
Verify: new chat → @mention Meltwater (or + → More → Meltwater) → ask "List actions" or "List my saved Meltwater searches." A list coming back confirms the connection.3
Internal only — for you, not the customer

Customer vs. employee connector. Customers connect to Meltwater — that's what the steps above and the email below reference. Meltwater employees connect to Meltwater Internal instead (installed automatically for ChatGPT Enterprise; added as a connector in Claude). For your own demo account, core tools are on; request add-on tools in the MCP Governance portal. To demo with a shared team account's assets, switch your default account under Account → Profile, then log out and back in to the assistant.410

Then get them to a fast "aha" — start with a skill

The connection is live. Before any open-ended prompting, run a Meltwater skill together: one ask, and a finished, presentation-ready report built from their own data.10

Start with a Meltwater skill. Have the customer type these two prompts, then read the report together and point out the citations: "What Meltwater skills are available?" "Run the Brand Pulse skill for [company]." Brand Pulse is the default first run. Coverage Report, Competitive SOV, and Issues & Crisis are one ask away — pick the one that matches the team. Nothing to upload: the skills come through the Meltwater connector. Details in Skills & extending it.
Then show open prompting. Once a skill has landed, show that they can ask anything — the way they'd ask a colleague: "Give me a morning overview of coverage about [company] and flag anything that needs attention. Cluster the results by story and give me some high-level stats about each cluster." Follow up in the same chat — "drill into that cluster," then "compare it to last week." More starters by team in Deeper workflows.
Best practice, once they're hooked — add brand context. After a few live wins, set up the Brand DNA skill so the customer's brand context (purpose, positioning, tone, audiences) applies automatically to every answer, and reports reflect the brand instead of a generic read. It's the natural next step, not the first thing to configure — setup is in Skills & extending it.2
Handoff — leave them self-sufficient. Before you close out the session, confirm: the user has connected and verified independently; they've run one Meltwater skill and at least two prompts that matter to their role; they know how to check usage by asking "What's my credit usage?"; they know results follow their own Meltwater permissions and workspace assignments — they see exactly what they'd see in the platform, nothing more; and they know who their admin is for adding more users. Then set the expectation: "Ask it the way you'd ask a colleague, and follow up — each question builds on the last."

Put it to work

Deeper workflows by team

After the first skill, match open prompts to the team you're onboarding. The same prompts work in Claude and ChatGPT. Media Relations, Influencer (Klear), and GenAI Lens workflows light up for customers with those add-ons; add-on tools can reach ChatGPT later than Claude, so if one is missing there, reconnect the plugin and run that workflow in Claude for the demo.410

PR & Comms
"What coverage featured our brand this week?"
Also: what's the coverage on breaking news so far, and where? Is sentiment shifting, and what's driving it? Is an issue starting to spike?
Marketing & Insights
"How are we standing out from the competition?"
Also: what's our share of voice vs the competitors we name? Which themes are our competitive edge? How did the last campaign land against benchmarks?
Media Relations
"Which journalists can amplify our story?"
Also: who's covering this topic? What angle is likely to land? Does the pitch align with their audience?
Influencer Marketing
"Which influencers are best suited for this campaign?"
Also: who's driving the conversation in our space? What hashtags are trending? Are they the right fit?
Work from existing searches — shows the write capability
"Run my saved '[search name]' search and give me an overview of this week's coverage."
"Take my '[search name]' search, add [competitor] to the Boolean, and show me how the results change."
"Refine the Boolean in my '[search name]' search to exclude [term], then give me an overview of the cleaned-up results."

Want more starters? Meltwater's public blog has 55 prompts grouped by workflow.11

The real unlock is follow-ups. Because the assistant can call multiple Meltwater tools in one conversation, the customer can keep going: "Drill into that — top three outlets driving negative sentiment," then "now compare that to last month." It can also combine Meltwater data with the customer's own context and other connected tools in the same chat.4

Make it stick

Skills & extending it

The MCP connection is the access. A skill is the reusable recipe that tells the assistant how to use it well, every time — and Meltwater's own skills are the fastest route to a polished first deliverable.

The MCP connection — the access

Gets Meltwater into Claude or ChatGPT as a callable set of tools. Without it, the assistant has no Meltwater data to reach.

+

A skill — the recipe

A reusable set of instructions and reference material the assistant follows automatically whenever it's relevant, so the customer doesn't re-explain the job every time.

Two kinds of skills to show customers — ready-made ones from Meltwater, and ones they set up for their own brand:

Meltwater platform skills — ready-made, served through the connector

What's there: Brand Pulse (brand health infographic + PDF), Coverage Report (news and social with period-over-period comparison), Competitive SOV (benchmarks a brand against auto-researched competitors), and Issues & Crisis (an 11-section crisis brief). There's also Meltwater Knowledge, a primer that helps the assistant pick the right tools — it runs on its own.10

How customers use them: ask "What Meltwater skills are available?", then run one by name — "Run the Competitive SOV skill for [brand] against [competitor]." They're delivered through the Meltwater connector, so there's nothing to upload, and they follow the customer's entitlements.

When to introduce them: first, right after the connection is verified (Step 3) — they're the fastest route to an "aha."

Brand DNA — brand context skill

What it does: captures the brand's identity — purpose, positioning, tone of voice with real writing samples, do's and don'ts, audiences. Once installed, trend scoring, briefs, and content review reflect the brand instead of a generic read on the data.2

When to introduce it: once the customer has had a few wins with Meltwater skills and live prompts — it's a best-practice upgrade, not first-run setup.

Setup (Claude): Customize → Skills → Add → upload the Brand DNA skill file → fill in the template with the customer's details (Claude can walk them through it section by section). After that, just mentioning the brand in a question ("How did we do this week?") applies it automatically — no special syntax.2

Setup (ChatGPT): skills upload under Plugins → Skills → Upload. A custom GPT or project instructions loaded with the same brand files is the fallback.10

The library grows over time. Enablement has more skills planned after the first batch, alongside an MCP Playbook (setup video, Brand DNA, building your own skill). Check the MCP Saleshood page for the latest before promising a specific skill.10
ChatGPT vs. Claude. Meltwater's platform skills come through the connector, so they should work in ChatGPT as well as Claude — confirm in a live demo first, since new and add-on tools can reach ChatGPT later than Claude.10
When it doesn't work

Troubleshooting & known limits

The failures below are the ones that actually come up in the field, mapped to the fix. Most are access-sequence issues, not real bugs.2310

"Meltwater doesn't show up in my connector / plugin list."
Almost always the account: confirm they're signed in to the work / enterprise account, not a personal one. If it's still missing, the org-level connector or plugin probably isn't enabled yet (Step 2) — loop in the champion and their IT admin.
"I only see 'Browse', not 'Add' custom connector in Claude settings."
Expected on Enterprise plans — only Org Owners can add custom connectors. For the official Meltwater connector this doesn't matter; the org admin enables it once for everyone. This trips people up when they try to self-add a custom connection instead of using the enabled one.
"Sign-in fails with access_denied, 'no seat,' or 'connection canceled.'"
Check three things: the plugin/connector is enabled at the workspace level; the user has an active Meltwater seat and signs in with the same business email as that seat; and the account actually has an MCP credits entitlement — without it, sign-in fails. If all three check out, file a Support ticket.10
"I get a login error signing in to Meltwater."
Sign-in runs through the organization's SSO — confirm the Meltwater account is active and that the user's SSO is set up. If SSO fails, it's a Meltwater admin / Support issue, not a connector one.
"The response came back empty or errored."
Usually an expired sign-in — reconnect the connector/plugin to re-trigger the sign-in flow. In ChatGPT, if a write action specifically is blocked, the workspace may be set to read-only for Meltwater; that's an admin setting. An expired-token error that reconnecting doesn't clear is a Support ticket.10
"Do users have to sign in every session?"
No — they sign in once and the connection stays live. Re-authentication only happens under normal conditions: their SSO session or security policy requires a fresh sign-in, an admin changes or revokes their access, or they connect a new assistant. It's the same SSO flow each time, so an occasional re-auth is expected, not a fault.4
"ChatGPT says 'tool not found,' or I see two Meltwater plugins."
A known ChatGPT-side issue. Start a new chat, select Meltwater with the + icon rather than relying on auto-routing, and make sure the user is connected to only one Meltwater plugin. If it persists, disconnect and reconnect.10
"How do we see how many credits we've used?"
Two ways. Any user can ask the assistant "What's my credit usage?" — it can break usage down by month, tool, user, or the current conversation. Account admins also get an MCP usage dashboard on the Account page: credits remaining, usage trend, usage by user, and top tools. Usage by Meltwater employees never counts against the customer's credits.12
"Why did a short prompt use so many credits?"
Credits aren't tokens. They measure the Meltwater work the assistant requests, not the length of the question. A four-word prompt that triggers several data-heavy tools can cost more than a long prompt that calls none. The analysis and writing the assistant does afterward costs no credits.4
"Can we give MCP to only some of our users?"
Not today. Everyone with a Meltwater seat on the account gets MCP, and their platform permissions carry over. Role-based access is on the roadmap — log the request in #product-feedback.4
"Can we connect a different assistant — Copilot, Gemini, Perplexity?"
Usually yes. Claude, ChatGPT, and Grok list Meltwater in their own catalogs. Most other MCP-compatible assistants — Microsoft Copilot (via Copilot Studio), Gemini, Perplexity, Cursor — connect through a custom connection URL where the customer's IT policy allows it, and the developer portal has a connection guide for each. Dedicated Microsoft Copilot and Cowork connectors are expected in 2027. If an assistant isn't listed, search its docs for "MCP" — if it supports MCP connections, it can likely connect. The usual blocker is IT policy, not a technical limit.413
What to set expectations on — not supported today
  • Raw mention exports. It returns AI-generated, cited responses — not raw article lists. For raw dataset access, use the Meltwater Insights API.4
  • Tagging, bulk scoring, or writing back to Meltwater fields. Sentiment analysis can happen in the assistant, but it can't overwrite Meltwater's fields, and tagging isn't supported (it's on the roadmap). For high-volume enrichment, use the Insights API with automations.4
  • Always-on real-time streaming. Request/response by design — the assistant has to invoke a tool. For near-real-time delivery, use Insights API Data Streams.4
  • Full Explore+ custom categories & filter sets. The standard tools reach the regular Explore index; deeper custom-index work (custom categories, filter sets) isn't fully reachable via the MCP. Custom fields are visible but you can't extract deeper insights from them.10
  • Owned social (Measure) analytics. Owned-channel tools aren't available and there's no ETA — don't position MCP for owned-channel reporting.10
  • Every premium source. Licensed content generally follows the customer's subscription, but some licensed providers are excluded or limited in MCP results, and citation links may land on the outlet's login page.4
  • Building a product on journalist or influencer data. Using MCP output to power a product or service for third parties is barred by Meltwater's and Klear's terms.4
