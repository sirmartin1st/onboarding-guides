Digital-First onboarding playbook

How to run a Digital-First onboarding (also called Digital MM or DOBD) from the day the account lands to CSM handoff. Set up the account behind the scenes, let the automated comms drive the early weeks, and step in at the day 15, 21 and 30 checkpoints when usage health says to.

Internal only. Do not share with customers.

How this motion works

Digital-First is a scaled onboarding for customers with straightforward use cases. Instead of a kickoff call and 1-1 trainings, customers get unlimited live group trainings, weekly Office Hours and workshops, the New to Meltwater checklist and on-demand courses. You handle the setup behind the scenes and step in when risk or no usage shows up.

We are not ignoring these customers. They still have an IM and a CSM, and they get automated comms from day one. The goal is to get them into the one-to-many programs before any 1-1 time. It's modeled on the Pooled SMB cohort, where new customers reach 70%+ active users in 30 days, with usage on par with the MM and SMB average, without 1-1 trainings.

Every English-speaking onboarding under $35k ACV that isn't Pooled and doesn't include Klear follows this motion. Pooled accounts follow a separate process and aren't covered here.

The target is a closed-out onboarding by about day 45, with green accounts handed to the CSM from day 30. You and the CSM stay close from day 30 to 45, so nothing surprises either of you at day 45. A red account should be flagged to your manager before day 45, and the skill flags anything still open past day 48.

01

First-time setup with /digital-signals

Do this once. After that, you'll get your most urgent items in Slack every weekday morning.

Paste this into Claude (Cowork) to schedule the run. Claude sets it up for you.

Schedule prompt
Every weekday at 7:30am run /digital-signals and send me a slack DM (not a channel post) of the most urgent items to address today

The Totango feed refreshes between 5 and 6 am Eastern, so don't schedule it earlier than 6:30 am Eastern. The skill needs the Google Drive and Gmail connectors, and it has to be switched on under Customize, then Skills.

What the skill does

/digital-signals reads your accounts from the Totango digital feed and gives you two short lists: what to fix in Meltwater today, and who to reach out to, with drafts for the customer emails and handoff notes. It never sends anything, so every action is your call. Digest warnings are a prompt to review and tune the digest, not a verdict, because you know the account best. Pooled accounts show up in their own section of the output, and they follow a different process.

02

Receiving a new account

The Meltwater setup comes first, because it's what the customer sees. Then the Totango and Salesforce admin. Aim to finish both by day 3.

Part A: Set up the account in Meltwater

Only build what the customer purchased. Use the Meltwater temp login link from the /digital-signals output. The VoCAgent brief in Slack is the best starting point for what to build.

  1. Build starter searches with a digest and/or alerts

    Build a brand search, one for each key competitor, and an industry or topic search. Tie a daily digest and/or alerts to them. The boolean examples guide has patterns to start from.

    Done when: the searches exist and a digest or alerts are attached. By day 3

  2. Build at least one starter dashboard

    Build it on the customer's main topic while you're already in the account. It's easier than coming back later.

    Done when: a dashboard exists. By day 3

  3. Create at least one MIRA project

    Set up a starter project the customer can build on.

    Done when: a MIRA project exists. By day 3

  4. GenAI Lens, if they have it: create at least 12 prompts

    Skip this step if GenAI Lens isn't in their package.

    Done when: 12 or more prompts exist. By day 3

  5. Newsletter, if they have it: create a branded template

    Skip this step if Newsletter isn't in their package.

    Done when: a branded template exists. By day 3

  6. Activate users as close to the contract start date as possible

    Get the user list from the customer. An automated email asks the contract signer for it, and if they don't reply, send the "info needed" email from the reference section. Add users from the user setup sheet, and check its New User Submission tab regularly for new submissions. /digital-signals also flags possible matches. Do not activate users ahead of the contract start date without permission.

    Done when: users are activated on or right after the start date. By day 3

Part B: Admin in Totango and Salesforce

The /digital-signals output links to each account's Totango plan page. Salesforce links go to the opportunity. Customer emails are logged automatically, so there's nothing to log there.

  1. Activate the "Digital-First Onboarding" SuccessPlay

    Activate the SuccessPlay and complete the first task.

    Done when: the SuccessPlay is active and the first task is checked. Day 1

  2. Make sure the CSM is listed as the Owner

    The CSM must be listed as the Owner on the account in Totango. Without it, the automated campaigns do not run. If the Owner is blank or wrong, flag the MD. The skill also flags a missing CSM from day 3.

    Done when: the Owner field shows the CSM. Day 1

  3. Confirm the VoCAgent output with the new biz rep and the CSM

    Send a short Slack message to the new biz rep and the CSM, asking whether the VoCAgent output is accurate. Where their answers and the brief disagree, go with them.

    Done when: the rep or CSM has confirmed, or you've corrected the brief. Before day 3

  4. Fill in the plan summary

    On the Plan tab, add the VoCAgent output to the Plan Summary field, with any corrections from the rep and CSM. Keep what the customer bought separate from what was only discussed in sales.

    Done when: the plan summary is filled in. By day 3

  5. Apply the right media monitoring objective(s)

    Some objectives are added automatically based on the products the customer bought, like the GenAI Lens one. Your job is to apply the right media monitoring objective(s) for what the customer bought and why: Brand Monitoring, Campaign Management, Competitive Benchmarking, Content and Campaign Optimization, or Market and Industry Research. Milestones update automatically as the customer hits certain usage patterns, so there's nothing to customize.

    Done when: the right media monitoring objective(s) are applied. By day 3

  6. Flag the champion in Salesforce

    Tag the working-level champion on the opportunity. Watch this walkthrough video for how. The skill tags accounts with no champion from day 7.

    Done when: Champion on Working Level reads Yes. By day 7

Before the start date to day 3

Set up the account

The goal is a working account by day 3, with the Meltwater setup and the Totango admin done. Every later check assumes the basics are there.

What happens automatically
  • After order approval, the contract signer gets a welcome email asking them to submit users.
  • At the contract start date, customers get a welcome email with the New User Checklist, and a short user intent survey appears in the app.
  • Day 3: an email asks the customer to review the searches and digest you built, and to log in to Meltwater.
What /digital-signals flags
  • No searches, no digest, or no users shows up under Fix today.
  • A possible new user submission, to confirm on the user setup sheet.
  • From day 3, a missing CSM is flagged to the MD.
  • If the contract hasn't started yet, nothing is raised.
What to do
  • Build the account in Meltwater first. Searches with a digest or alerts, a dashboard, a MIRA project, plus GenAI Lens prompts and a newsletter template if they have them. See Part A.
  • Activate users as close to the start date as you can. Never before it without permission. Check the user setup sheet for new submissions.
  • Do the Totango admin. Activate the SuccessPlay, confirm the VoCAgent output with the rep and CSM, and fill in the plan summary. See Part B.
  • Check the day 1 and day 3 tasks in the SuccessPlay.
  • Check the CSM. The CSM must be listed as the Owner in Totango, or the automated campaigns won't run. If it's missing by day 3, flag the MD.
Days 4 to 13

Automated start

Automated emails drive customers to the New to Meltwater User Checklist, live trainings and Office Hours. No customer emails are triggered from you in this window, so use it to close gaps and finish the admin.

What happens automatically
  • Day 7: an email asks the customer to sign up for live training, with a link to register for a workshop.
  • Day 10, GenAI Lens customers only: an email asks them to set up GenAI Lens.
What /digital-signals flags
  • From day 7: no champion, or a blank onboarding stage.
  • From day 7: digest too empty, noisy or flooding.
  • Through day 14: only one user set up.
  • Watching list: yellow or red accounts from day 7 to 13.
What to do
  • If a customer asks for a 1-1 before day 15, use the objection templates below before you give up your time. The point is to get them into trainings and Office Hours first.
  • Finish the admin. Flag the champion by day 7. If the right objectives aren't applied yet, do that now. The skill links to each.
  • Monitor setup and product usage. Check that the customer has logged in and is using what you built.
  • Review the digest. If it's blank most mornings, add an industry topic. If it's flooding, tighten the query. Use your judgment, since the size is an estimate.
  • Start digging into Watching accounts. Look at which products are red or yellow, so you know what you'd fix on day 15.
When a customer replies
  • Answer their question first.
  • Drive them straight to the platform to do the task, like adding user seats or building a report.
  • If they ask for time and the account is green, point them to CSM Office Hours or a workshop. Suggest a 1-1 only as a last resort, and passively.

Days 14 to 20

Day 15 check

Usage health decides what happens. Green accounts with setup complete need nothing from you yet. Yellow or red accounts, or accounts where setup isn't done, get a fix and a tailored email, sent around day 15 or 16.

What happens automatically
  • Day 14, Media Relations customers only: an email invites them to join Office Hours.
What /digital-signals flags
  • Yellow or red usage health at day 14 to 20.
  • If you already emailed this account in the last 14 days, it moves to Watching instead.
  • A "Fix first" note means setup work is still open on the account.
What to do
  • Reach out if there's no readership or Media Intelligence usage. That's the trigger in the motion, and it usually shows up as yellow or red health. The skill's roster shows readership for each account.
  • Do any "Fix first" item. The email assumes the account is set up properly.
  • Find what's dragging health. Look at the product status in the skill's roster, and lead with the weakest product the customer bought.
  • Build the fix. If anything from the day 3 setup is missing, like the dashboard, build it now. The draft assumes it's done.
  • Send a tailored email. Edit the draft below, and fill the [brackets].
  • If the account is red or yellow, offer 1-1 training.
  • Check the day 15 task in the SuccessPlay.
Make the email specific
  • Dashboards: if the account has one or more, write "I set up a dashboard for you on [topic]". The skill can prove it exists, but the topic is yours to name.
  • Users who haven't logged in: check Totango for the names, then mention them. Never guess names.
  • No community activity: point them to Office Hours and the New to Meltwater resources.

Need ideas for moving a specific metric? The usage health guide lists tactics by product.

Day 21 check

You're close to the end of the onboarding period, so this is where unused products matter. A yellow or red account gets an email that asks for a call and names what hasn't been used.

What happens automatically

  • Day 25, Klear customers only: an email asks them to set up Klear.

Days 30 to 32

Day 30 check

This is the decision point. Green accounts move to the CSM. Yellow or red accounts get one more push for training before onboarding closes.

What happens automatically
  • Every account gets a completion email from the CSM, inviting them to expand their use case. The timing varies with the account's health score.
What /digital-signals flags
  • Green at day 30 or later: hand off to the CSM. This holds even past day 48.
  • Yellow or red at day 30 to 32: ask for a call.
Green account: hand off
  • Fix first. If a digest or other gap is open, close it so the CSM doesn't inherit it.
  • Make sure the admin is done. The right media monitoring objective(s) applied (milestones update on their own), and the champion flagged.
  • Send the CSM a handoff note. Fill in every line from Totango and your call notes. Leave a line out only if the data is missing, never guess.
  • Check the day 30 task, then the last task in the SuccessPlay. Checking the last task marks onboarding complete in Totango.
  • The CSM takes it from there. They email the customer to ask for the account health check (template below), then run the mini Account Health Check: a usage summary, org chart and user roles, key use cases, and business goals.

Days 33 to 47

Day 45 check

The account is past the standard window, and it isn't green yet. The goal is the best health you can get by day 45, with 1-1 training as the main lever.

What /digital-signals flags
  • Yellow or red usage health at day 33 to 47.
  • Green accounts still go to the CSM.
What to do
  • Stay in step with the CSM. Between day 30 and day 45, talk regularly about where the account stands. There should be no surprises at day 45.
  • If the account is still red as day 45 approaches, tell your manager ahead of time that it may not close. Don't wait for the day 48 flag.
  • If it reaches yellow by day 45, you can typically close it. Align with the CSM first.
  • Keep pushing for a 1-1 training. Use the day 30 email, adjusted for what you know about the account since.
  • Work the weakest products. Pick the one or two that would move health most, and aim the training at them.
  • Hand off as soon as it turns green. Don't hold a green account to finish the window. Check the last SuccessPlay task when you do.
  • Check the day 45 task in the SuccessPlay.
Day 48 and later

Past target

An account still open at day 48 needs a decision, not more of the same outreach.

What /digital-signals flags
  • Any non-green account at day 48 or later goes to your manager.
  • Green accounts still get the CSM handoff.
What to do
  • Flag it to your manager. The skill marks this as flag-only, so there's no draft.
  • Close it out, or document why it's still open. Put the reason in Totango.
  • Hand off if it's green. Take the win, even this late.
