Standing up Klear for your customer

A tactical runbook for getting a Klear account from "contract signed" to "client running campaigns" — activation, account configuration, the ticket-gated features (IG Story Tracking and Klear Pay), and how to enable the client on the tool.

Klear in 60 seconds

The customer already bought the value. Your job is correct execution: get the account live, configured to their workflow, and the team enabled. This is a map of where everything lands.

What Klear is: Meltwater's influencer marketing platform — a database of 30M+ creators plus the tooling to discover and vet them, manage a creator community, pay creators, and report on campaign ROI.9 Everything you configure in setup exists to serve one of the four use cases below.

Klear breaks into four customer use cases. Knowing which ones a customer cares about tells you what to configure and where to push adoption:3

🔍

Grow your community

Discover, vet, and recruit creators — search by topic, keyword, or visual, then vet for brand fit. The use case most clients adopt on their own.

💬

Manage your community

Contract and communicate with creators in one inbox, run campaign briefs and content approvals, and store first-party data. A key stickiness driver.

💳

Pay your community

Klear Pay — pay creators directly through Klear via Gigapay or Tipalti. Lower adoption, but clients with integrated payments have far higher retention.

📊

Report & analyse

Track content, calculate ROI and EMV, compare market by market, and pull competitive intelligence. Configuration-heavy — get the settings right early.

In-tool, the four use cases become one client workflow: the client works left to right across the tabs. Everything you set up in configuration feeds this flow.

Scope of this guide: the IM path — how to activate, configure, and enable a client on Klear. It is not a client-facing user manual (send clients to Get Started with Klear and Klear Academy) and it does not cover the sales / lead-approval process.
Before you activate

Know the package before you touch the account

The package the customer bought decides what you can configure — number of workspaces, whether the API and automated Story Tracking are included, and which Klear Pay vendor is available. Confirm it first (check Totango / the contract), then set up to match.4

Klear sells in four tiers. Rather than memorize seat and influencer caps (those live in the contract and change), think in terms of what unlocks as you move up:4

Essentials (L1)
Entry — discover, manage, report
  • Discover, vet, and manage creators; report on campaign ROI
  • Full Recruit (landing page / website embed)
  • Self-serve unauthenticated IG Story Tracking (tagged, brand-connected)
  • eCommerce integrations (Shopify / WooCommerce), tracking pixels, coupon codes
  • Gigapay available as an optional add-on
Advanced (L2)
Adds payments, automation, API
  • Everything in Essentials, plus:
  • Klear Pay via Tipalti (pay creators from the Payments module)
  • Meltwater-assisted automated IG Story Tracking (needs Support activation)
  • Influencer Campaign API (push campaign data to BI tools)
  • More seats and active influencers
Advanced (L3)
Adds scale & a second workspace
  • Everything in Advanced (L2), plus:
  • Multiple workspaces — segment by team, region, or brand
  • Higher seat and active-influencer allocations
Enterprise (L4)
Top of the range
  • Everything in Advanced (L3), plus:
  • The most workspaces, seats, and active influencers
  • Best fit for multi-market / multi-team programs
Exact seat, influencer, and workspace numbers live in the contract / Totango — not here. They change with packaging updates and vary by deal. This guide stays qualitative on purpose; confirm the specifics per account before you promise anything.
Step one

Activate the account

Klear accounts are created by Product Support off an emailed request — there's no self-serve provisioning. It's a short, mechanical step, but it gates everything after it, so get the request right the first time.

Before you send the request: Support activates the account only once the order is Active in Salesforce, and not before the order start date. If Klear already shows on the account in Salesforce, it's cleared to set up.
1

Email help@meltwater.com to request activation

Send a request to help@meltwater.com asking Product Support to activate a Klear account for the customer.

2

Include everything Support needs

Provide the client's full name and email address. If there will be multiple users, name the primary admin. In the same request, ask for access for yourself and the accounts team by listing all of your emails.

3

Wait for confirmation

Product Support creates the account and confirms back when it's live. You can't move on to configuration until this lands.

Need your own access to demo? For a buddy / demo account, follow How Do I Get Access To Social Influencers. Access to a client's account comes through the same activation request above (list your emails when you email help@) — it then appears as a workspace option in your Meltwater logins.9

Step three

Feature activations that need a ticket or vendor

Two features aren't just settings — they need a Support ticket or a third-party vendor to switch on. These are the ones IMs most often trip over, so they get their own section.

A

Instagram Story Tracking

Support ticket required for L2+ & add-on

Instagram Stories are one of the biggest reporting gaps in influencer marketing — most platforms can't track them without the influencer authenticating. Klear captures them in three ways depending on plan and add-on:4

  • Self-serve, tagged (all plans): when the brand IG + Facebook are connected and an influencer tags the brand in a Story, the client gets notified and can save it to a campaign within 24 hours. No ticket needed.
  • Meltwater-assisted, automated (L2+): Meltwater captures and saves eligible tagged Stories to the campaign on the client's behalf, 7 days a week. Requires a Support ticket to activate.
  • Advanced manual, no tag required (add-on, any plan L1–L4): Meltwater manually gathers and saves Stories from an approved influencer list even when the brand isn't tagged. Purchased as an add-on; requires a ticket plus a completed tracking template.
The pre-launch checklist that actually matters. For assisted tracking, before any campaign goes live confirm: the client has connected the brand Instagram AND Facebook in Klear; influencers are added to the campaign(s); and the client has told influencers to tag the brand in their Stories. If these aren't done before launch, Stories won't be tracked or saved — there's no retroactive capture.4
◆
Assisted tracking (L2+)
Support ticket
  1. Submit a Support ticket requesting Instagram Story Tracking Support.
  2. Include the Salesforce account / opportunity link and the customer's plan (L2, L3, or add-on from L1).
  3. The account is added to the monitored list. You may be pulled into #manual-instagram-story-tracking for details.
  4. Complete the pre-launch checklist above before campaigns go live.
Metrics note: performance is estimated on Klear's True Reach — real Meta data only comes from influencers authenticated to Klear.
◈
Advanced manual add-on
Add-on + ticket
  1. Submit a ticket requesting Advanced Instagram Story Tracking Support; include the Salesforce link and which plan the add-on was bought from.
  2. Copy the tracking template, have the customer complete it, and return it before the campaign goes live.
  3. Link the completed details in the internal tracking doc.
  4. Meltwater monitors the approved list (up to ~20 influencers/day) and saves Stories to the campaign, 7 days a week.

B

Klear Pay — Gigapay vs Tipalti

Vendor onboarding required

Klear Pay lets clients pay creators directly from Klear. It runs through one of two vendors, and the client must contract with that vendor — payments can't be switched on without it. Adoption is lower than other use cases, but clients with integrated payments retain dramatically better, so it's worth leaning in.3

Gigapay
Optional on all plans · simpler setup
  • Merchant of Record — Gigapay handles tax, KYC, and compliance
  • Streamlined onboarding; often very straightforward for clients
  • Requires a separate agreement and an annual payment commitment
  • Good default when the client wants tax/compliance off their plate
Tipalti
L2+ · more configuration
  • Client is responsible for filing taxes (jurisdiction-dependent)
  • Funds sent to a Tipalti account; released to creators from Klear
  • Optional AP approval step for finance oversight
  • More setup steps and a longer onboarding than Gigapay
Both can power campaign work and affiliate programs. Pick the vendor with the client based on how much tax/compliance they want to own (Gigapay takes it on; Tipalti leaves it with the brand) and their plan level (Tipalti is L2+). For a client-ready side-by-side of the two partners, use the KlearPay partner comparison deck.10

Gigapay onboarding — step by step

Internal Klear contact for setup questions: Paul Davison. Gigapay-specific contact: Hjalmar Bergkvist Soto, or #product-gigapay.6

1

Pre-activation checklist

Confirm the client understands the volume-based pricing model, is willing to commit to the annual payment threshold, and understands Gigapay is the Merchant of Record (managing tax, KYC, compliance). If they're unsure which option fits, share the customer discussion deck.

2

Submit the activation ticket

Open a Support ticket for Klear activation and include the full business details: email, phone, TIN, VAT number, friendly/company name, registered legal name, country (3-letter ISO), state (if US/CA/AU), city, address, and ZIP. This routes straight to Gigapay's onboarding team.

3

Gigapay account setup

A Gigapay rep contacts the customer to run KYC verification, complete financial onboarding, finalize the Gigapay Service Agreement, and train them on funding the portal. Once signed, the account is created and configured.

4

Link the account in Klear

Klear Product Support syncs the Gigapay account to the workspace. It appears under Account Settings → Integrations → Payments. A client can connect multiple Gigapay accounts (each with its own balance/currency).

5

Campaign setup

In the Campaign Brief, toggle Add Payment Details. If there are multiple Gigapay accounts, select the right one — this can't be changed once the campaign is created. Invited influencers enter payment info in their creator portal, then finish onboarding via Gigapay's follow-up email. Status updates to "Influencer is payable."

6

Send payments

When deliverables are met, go to the campaign's Connect tab, choose Send Payment, enter the amount, review, and pay. The influencer claims the payout via Gigapay; funds release automatically. Track status in Connect → Payments and export summaries anytime.

Step four

Enable the client on Klear

Account's live and configured — now get the team using it. Enablement is where adoption is won or lost. Run working sessions around the client's workflows and outcomes, not feature tours.3

Move from "tell & show" to "do." Tell them what they need to know, show them how, then give them a task and get their hands on the keyboard. Reinforce with teachbacks — ask them to explain back what they just learned and walk you through the workflow themselves. Adjust depth to the persona: Specialists, Generalists, and Managers all use Klear differently.3

Enable in the order the client will actually work — the same left-to-right flow from the overview. Anchor each session on the outcome, not the tab:

1

Discover & vet (Grow your community)

Teach searching by topic, keyword, and visual, then vetting on engagement, audience demographics, and brand fit. This is the use case clients adopt most naturally — but if it's the only thing they use, that's a risk trigger. Show advanced search and vetting to deepen the value.3

2

Build the campaign (Report & analyse foundation)

Walk them through creating a campaign, adding members, and setting tracking keywords/hashtags/handles so content is captured correctly. Tie labels and tags back to how they want to report.

3

Collaborate in Connect (Manage your community)

Invite and brief creators, message in-platform, approve content, and store first-party data. This is a major stickiness driver — the more of their community management lives in Klear, the more reliant (and retained) they become.3

4

Pay creators (Pay your community)

If Klear Pay is set up, enable them on sending payments and tracking status. Don't let this land in the "too hard" basket — integrated payments correlate with far higher retention, so coach through it during enablement.3

5

Measure & report (Report & analyse)

Show the Measure tab (Overview vs Analytics), building a campaign report, and — for L2+ — competitive intelligence via Monitors and the Influencer Campaign API for BI dashboards. Make sure the output matches what the report recipient actually needs.3

Know when a client is actually enabled. Look for leading indicators per use case, not just logins: influencers uploaded and assigned to campaigns, campaigns with tracking configured, Connect being used for real communication, first-party data captured, and reports being pulled. Usage across multiple use cases — especially Manage and Pay — is the strongest signal of a sticky, retained account.3

When you get stuck

Troubleshooting, FAQs & resources

The questions that come up most in the field, plus where to route issues and find the deeper collateral.9

"My client can't find a specific influencer in Klear."
Klear is a search engine — get creative with inputs: different languages, keywords like "brand ambassador", hashtags, similar brands, or uploaded images. Also check account type: Instagram profiles must be Business or Creator accounts and public to be searchable. New influencers can be added manually via the Profiles tab.
"We pulled data from an influencer before, now there's nothing — but they're posting."
They likely switched to a Personal account (not Creator or Business). If follower data doesn't mirror native, force a refresh: open the influencer profile → the arrows next to Edit Social Networks (gear icon).
"Why is there no demographic or brand-safety data on this influencer?"
For unauthenticated profiles, Klear infers demographics from audience data — but if there isn't enough to pass the accuracy threshold, those fields stay blank. Authenticated influencers (connected to Klear) return richer, verified data.
"Why is True Reach higher than the follower count?"
True Reach is Klear's proprietary estimate of how many people actually see content on average across platforms (everything except YouTube). It's not follower count — content can reach beyond a creator's direct followers, so the two legitimately differ.
"How do we track sales / conversions?"
Only where links or coupon codes are generated in Klear — otherwise there's no way to tie it back. Depending on the store, you'll integrate conversion tracking (Shopify/WooCommerce, or the manual product catalog for others). If the client uses another system, they can copy links into Klear to generate trackable bit.ly links.
"Something's broken / the client hit an error."
Have the client (or you) file a Support ticket with the specific URL(s). For urgent issues, escalate the ticket internally and share the ticket number in the Klear field channel for feedback. Search the field channel first — most questions have already been answered.
! Set expectations on these
  • IG Stories need tags (or the add-on). Assisted tracking only captures Stories where the influencer tags the brand. No-tag capture requires the Advanced manual add-on. Real Meta metrics only come from authenticated influencers.
  • Campaign size. The max is contract-dependent, but recommend no more than ~500 influencers per campaign for the best UX and load times.
  • Data freshness. Campaigns update roughly every 24 hours; link-click data comes ~24 hours after via bit.ly. You can force-update an individual influencer from their profile.
  • Red / Xiaohongshu. Klear monitors public Red profiles in Discovery only — clients manage those partnerships natively, outside Klear.
